    How the pipeline decides
        
      
          
    1. Scene: the empty beaker = a bright percentile of ~100 frames; the waterline = the strongest horizontal edge; the ROI = where anything moved.
      2. 
          
    2. Tracking: every frame minus the background; the dark blob that overlaps last frame's fish is the fish; an ellipse gives its length and tilt, the darkest spot its eye.
      3. 
          
    3. Features: positions smoothed over features.smooth_s, then speed, turning, depth below the waterline and posture per 1 s bin, in body lengths (BL).
      4. 
          
    4. Labels: rules in priority order (untracked, surface, LORR, freeze), the rest split into controlled / erratic by a model fitted on all subjects; short bouts merged.
      5. 
          
    5. Saved: one final pass over the whole track, identical to python -m fishbehavior all.
